ABOUT THE PROJECT

One World, One Life

One World, One Life is an independent informational project designed to make global crises easier to understand, explore, and follow.

Our Purpose

Events around the world can be difficult to understand when information is spread across many different organizations, reports, and news sources. One World, One Life brings crisis information, country context, humanitarian conditions, and relevant sources together in one accessible place.

The goal is to help visitors understand what is happening, where it is happening, why it matters, and where they can find reliable information or established humanitarian organizations responding to a crisis.

What the Site Covers

The interactive map documents major ongoing or recent global crises and connects them with country profiles that provide geographic, political, humanitarian, and crisis-related context.

Armed Conflict

Wars, major armed confrontations, and other significant organized conflicts.

Humanitarian Crisis

Emergencies involving severe humanitarian needs, including food insecurity, health emergencies, and threats to civilian populations.

Displacement Crisis

Situations involving substantial refugee, asylum, or internal displacement.

Natural Disaster

Major earthquakes, floods, cyclones, and other disasters with significant human or humanitarian impacts.

A crisis can belong to more than one category when several dimensions of an emergency overlap.

Country Profiles

Country profiles provide additional context for understanding events on the map. Profiles can include population, geography, languages, currency, government structure, national leadership, United Nations status, humanitarian conditions, displacement information, recent history, and related crises.

A country may also be connected to a crisis when it is directly affected, is a party to a conflict, has significant military involvement, experiences a major humanitarian or refugee impact, or has another important diplomatic or humanitarian role.

A relationship shown between a country and a crisis provides context. It does not by itself assign responsibility, blame, or support to that country.

Sources & Accuracy

One World, One Life uses source links throughout the project so visitors can examine information beyond the summaries shown on the site.

Sources may include United Nations agencies, international humanitarian organizations, official institutions, governments, and other established organizations relevant to a particular crisis or country profile.

The project aims to present information accurately, clearly, and as neutrally as possible. The inclusion of a source does not mean that every statement or position of that organization is endorsed by One World, One Life.

Humanitarian Aid

One World, One Life does not collect or process donations.

Humanitarian aid links direct visitors to external organizations involved in humanitarian work. They are provided to make it easier for visitors who want to help to find established organizations responding to an emergency.

Visitors should review an organization's own information, policies, and donation options before contributing. External organizations operate independently from One World, One Life.

Neutrality

One World, One Life is intended as an informational resource. The project does not advocate for a government, political party, armed organization, or side in a conflict.

Describing a conflict, government, organization, territorial claim, humanitarian condition, or country's involvement in a crisis does not constitute an endorsement or political judgment.

Geographic boundaries are displayed for reference and do not imply a position on sovereignty, recognition, borders, or territorial disputes.

A Changing World

Global events can change quickly. Political leadership, conflict conditions, humanitarian needs, displacement figures, casualty estimates, and disaster impacts can change after information is published.

Where available, profiles include source links and verification dates to help visitors understand when information was last reviewed. One World, One Life will continue to be updated as the project develops and as reliable new information becomes available.
